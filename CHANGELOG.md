## [0.1.0] - unreleased

### Added

- OpenTherm boilers and room thermostats read through an OpenTherm Gateway (OTGW) of the local network, through its serial port bridged on TCP: the "Legacy TCP port 25238" of a NodoShop OTGW board, or ser2net for a USB or serial gateway; firmware and gateway / monitor mode read at each connection.
- A boiler device and a room thermostat device whose features are the OpenTherm values actually exchanged: flame, heating and hot water active, fault and its details (code, low water pressure, gas or flame, air pressure, water too hot), modulation, flow, return, hot water, outside and exhaust temperatures, flow temperature asked and maximum, water pressure, burner and pump starts and hours, room temperature and setpoint; a new value seen adds its feature.
- Room setpoint from Gladys, imposed on the thermostat until its next program change or until cancelled (setting), hot water target (35 to 70 °C, at most once a minute) and hot water on/off.
- Dashboard widgets "Boiler" (temperatures, modulation, pressure, burner, heating, hot water, fault, imposed setpoint with a button to give the thermostat its program back) and "Boiler curves" (over 1, 3 or 7 days).
- Scene triggers: the boiler reports a fault (with its code and flags), the fault is cleared, the boiler asks for maintenance, the thermostat is disconnected, the gateway stops answering, the gateway answers again.
- Scene actions: impose a room setpoint, give the thermostat its program back, send the outside temperature (from a Gladys sensor or a weather service) or stop sending it, set the hot water on, off or back to the thermostat, set the maximum flow temperature.
- Configuration button "Test the gateway".
